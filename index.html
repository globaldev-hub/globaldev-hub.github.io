Make informed choices

Explore opportunities with care

Online learning and work opportunities can be useful, but not every website is trustworthy. Research each platform, read its terms, and avoid promises of guaranteed income.

🔎

Research First

Check the company, payment rules, user feedback, and withdrawal requirements before signing up.

🛡️

Protect Yourself

Never share your passwords or one-time verification codes. Be cautious when asked to pay to unlock supposed earnings.
