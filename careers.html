CV checklist

  • Target role
  • Clear summary
  • Skills with examples

Portfolio checklist

  • 3–5 strong projects
  • Problem and solution
  • Your role
  • Live demo or screenshots
  • Lessons learned

Interview checklist

  • Practice project explanations
  • Prepare real examples
  • Research the role
  • Prepare questions
  • Be accurate about experience
