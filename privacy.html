Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GlobalDev Hub handles information when you visit and use this website.

GlobalDev Hub is a technology and learning website providing practical guides, online tools, resources, career information, and other useful digital content.

Information and website operation

GlobalDev Hub is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored by GitHub for security purposes.

For more information about how GitHub handles information, please review GitHub's privacy documentation.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact GlobalDev Hub by email, we may receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, name, and the contents of your message.

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, provide support, consider feedback, and communicate with you about your request.

Cookies and advertising

At the time of this policy update, GlobalDev Hub does not directly operate a personalized advertising system on this website.

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data collection, cookies, technologies, and third-party services.

Third-party advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies when advertisements are displayed. For example, Google requires publishers using its advertising products to clearly disclose relevant cookie and data practices in their privacy policies.

Third-party services

GlobalDev Hub may link to or use third-party websites and services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

How information is used

Information provided directly to GlobalDev Hub may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and messages.
  • Provide support related to the website.
  • Review suggestions and feedback.
  • Maintain and improve the website.
  • Protect the website and its users from abuse or security problems.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

GlobalDev Hub is intended to provide general technology, learning, career, and digital resources. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children through this website.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services, advertising, analytics, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
