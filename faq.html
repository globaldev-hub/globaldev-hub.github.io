🌍 About GlobalDev Hub

What is GlobalDev Hub?

GlobalDev Hub is a digital platform designed to bring together useful tools, learning resources, digital products and opportunities for developers, students, creators and digital professionals.

Who can use GlobalDev Hub?

Anyone interested in discovering digital tools, improving their skills, exploring resources or finding useful digital products can explore the public areas of the platform.

Do I need an account to browse the website?

Public pages and resources can be explored without an account. Some account-related features may require registration and sign-in.

🧰 Tools and Resources

Are the tools free to use?

Tools available on the platform may have different usage conditions. Check each tool and any third-party provider's terms before using it.

How can I suggest a useful resource?

Visit our Submit a Resource page and provide the resource details for consideration. Submissions are subject to review.

Can I recommend a developer tool or learning website?

Yes. Use the resource submission page to suggest relevant tools, tutorials, courses, documentation and learning websites.

🛍️ Digital Products and Store

What can I find in the Digital Store?

The store is designed to showcase digital products such as CV templates, e-books, digital guides, website templates and business or social media templates.

Can I purchase digital products right now?

Product availability and checkout activation depend on the individual product listing. A product marked as coming soon is not yet available for purchase.

Which currencies will be supported?

The digital product experience is being prepared to display prices in Nigerian naira (NGN) and US dollars (USD). Actual payment options will depend on the payment provider and supported account arrangements.

How do I get help with a product?

Use the Contact page to make an enquiry. Product-specific support and delivery details should be provided on each active listing.

🤝 Selling, Affiliates and Advertising

Can I sell my digital products through GlobalDev Hub?

Creators can express interest through the Sell With Us page. Seller onboarding, approval, payment and delivery arrangements must be confirmed before products can be listed for sale.

Does GlobalDev Hub use affiliate links?

The platform may feature affiliate links where applicable. An affiliate commission may be earned when visitors make qualifying purchases through those links. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

Can a business advertise on GlobalDev Hub?

Businesses and brands can express advertising interest through the Advertise With Us page. Advertising availability and terms require confirmation.

How are sponsored recommendations identified?

Sponsored placements and affiliate relationships should be clearly disclosed so visitors can distinguish promotional content from independent recommendations.

💚 Donations, Accounts and Privacy

Can I support GlobalDev Hub?

Yes. Visitors who wish to support the platform can use the Donate page. Donations are voluntary and separate from digital product purchases.

Is my personal information safe?

We aim to handle personal information responsibly. Read our Privacy Policy for information about privacy and data handling.

How can I contact the team?

Visit the Contact page for available contact options and enquiries.

Still need help?

If your question is not covered here, visit our Contact page. We welcome feedback and suggestions that can make GlobalDev Hub better.
